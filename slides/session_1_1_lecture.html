Core idea

How LLMs Actually Work (15 min)

  • The Predictive Autocomplete Analogy: Most business professionals mistakenly believe ChatGPT searches an encyclopedia database or "thinks" like a human. In reality, Large Language Models predict the next most likely word token based on statistical patterns learned from vast training datasets. When an LLM generates text, it calculates probability distributions across billions of parameters. Because it generates text probabilistically rather than retrieving hard records, it produces fluent language, but has no inherent sense of truth.
Core idea

ChatGPT vs. Google Search - The Strategic Distinction (15 min)

  • Decision Framework: Teach students when to search vs. when to prompt.
  • Use Traditional Search (Google): For verifiable historical facts, live stock quotes, statutory regulations, telephone numbers, and cited scientific research.
  • Use Generative AI (ChatGPT): For drafting executive correspondence, translating tone (e.g., technical jargon to layperson English), summarizing unformatted text, and brainstorming strategic angles.
  • The Modern Business Hybrid Workflow: (1) Gather verified facts from primary company sources -> (2) Feed those verified facts into ChatGPT to draft and format the communication.

Core idea

The Hallucination Problem & Enterprise Risk (10 min)

  • What is a Hallucination? Plausibly sounding, highly confident statements generated by AI that are completely false. Case Study - Air Canada Lawsuit (2024): An Air Canada customer service chatbot invented a non-existent bereavement refund policy. A customer relied on the chatbot's advice and sued when the refund was refused. The Canadian tribunal ruled Air Canada legally liable for its chatbot's hallucination. Managerial Golden Rule: AI is a brilliant junior copywriter, but a terrible factual auditor. Never approve an AI-generated document without personally verifying dates, names, and calculations.
Core idea

Instructor Live Demonstration Script & Discussion (10 min)

  • Step 1 (Naive Request): Type "Write an apology email to an angry customer whose delivery was late." Note the empty platitudes ("We value your business") and lack of actionable next steps. Step 2 (Engineered Prompt): Type a structured prompt specifying Role ("Customer Support Lead"), Context ("4-day courier delay on subscription coffee"), Task ("Draft 3-paragraph email"), Constraints ("Acknowledge delay in sentence 1; offer $10 credit code [COFFEE10]; explain backup carrier policy in 2 bullets; under 150 words"). Discussion Question: "Why does setting a specific persona (like 'Senior Operations Lead') improve AI output quality?" (Answer: It narrows the model's vocabulary and probability distribution toward professional domain terminology).

Exercise 2: Try a small variation

  • Scenario: From the same Amazon Customer Reviews dataset, identify a complex negative review that cites multiple simultaneous operational failures (e.g., damaged product packaging, a delayed shipment, and an unresponsive delivery courier).
  • Task: Engineer an advanced prompt instructing ChatGPT to act as a Senior Customer Operations Lead.   • Deliverable: A two-part deliverable: (1) A polished, empathetic customer reply under 150 words, and (2) A 3-bullet internal operational escalation note.
