The situation

The problem

Hana runs a small tea and snack shop. Her sales grew from $20,000 to $22,000, a 10% increase. But at the end of the year, she has less money left than before. Her root problem is that she does not know why, and she is about to hire a part-time worker. Her idea is to ask ChatGPT. She asked, "My sales went up 10%. Is my shop doing better? Should I hire?" ChatGPT said, "Yes. 10% growth is strong. Hiring will help you grow." But the shop's profit actually fell. The AI looked only at sales because the prompt did not include the costs.

Part 1Session 3.1

Six money words you need first

Before you ask an AI about profit, you must know what each number means. Each example uses Year A.

Key idea

Revenue

All the money customers paid. Year A: $20,000.

Key idea

Cost of goods sold (COGS)

What the shop paid for the tea and snacks it sold. Year A: $12,000.

Key idea

Gross profit

Revenue minus COGS. Year A: $20,000 - $12,000 = $8,000.

Part 1Session 3.1

Six money words you need first (continued)

Before you ask an AI about profit, you must know what each number means. Each example uses Year A.

Key idea

Gross margin

Gross profit as a percent of revenue. Year A: $8,000 / $20,000 = 40%. It shows how much of each dollar of sales is left after paying for the goods.

Key idea

Operating expenses (OPEX)

The other costs of running the shop, such as rent and wages. Year A: $5,000.

Key idea

Operating profit

Gross profit minus OPEX. Year A: $8,000 - $5,000 = $3,000. This is closer to the money the shop really keeps.

Part 2Session 3.1

Why the AI answer was wrong

"Sales went up" and "the shop earns more" are different questions. Compare the two years.

MeasureYear AYear BChange
Revenue$20,000$22,000+$2,000 (+10%)
Gross profit$8,000$7,700-$300
Gross margin40%35%-5 percentage points
Operating profit$3,000$2,500-$500
Key points

What the table shows

  • Sales went up by 10%, but operating profit went down by $500.
  • COGS grew faster than revenue, so each dollar of sales left less money: 40 cents in Year A and 35 cents in Year B.
  • A percentage point is the simple difference between two percents: 40% - 35% = 5 percentage points.

Part 3Session 3.1

How the three-step method fixes the answer

The instructor runs Poor Prompt 1 and Improved Prompts 1 and 2 below in front of the class and compares the answers.

Key idea

Step 1 fixes the missing-cost problem

With COGS, OPEX, and the formulas, the AI can see that profit fell.

Key idea

Step 2 fixes the trust problem

AI tools can make arithmetic mistakes, especially with percents. Checking two cells takes one minute.

Key idea

Step 3 fixes the guessing problem

The data shows that profit fell, not why it fell. Higher supplier prices or more discounts are possible explanations, so they must be labeled "Not verified."

What the AI often answers

A typical result

"Yes. 10% revenue growth is a strong result. Hiring a part-time worker can help you serve more customers and keep growing."

Why it is weak

Reasons

  • It gives only revenue, so the AI cannot see the costs or the profit.
  • It asks a yes/no business question before any numbers are checked.
  • The result is a confident "yes" to a decision that would make the shop's profit even smaller.

What the AI often answers

A typical result

"Your profit fell because your supplier raised prices. You should change suppliers and raise your prices by 10%."

Why it is weak

Reasons

  • It gives the numbers but no formulas, so the AI may calculate "profit" in a different way.
  • "Tell me exactly why" asks for a cause that the data does not show, so the AI invents one.
  • It asks for an action plan before the numbers are checked.

AI Chat
You
I run a small tea and snack shop. Here are two years of results in USD.
Year A: revenue 20,000; cost of goods sold (COGS) 12,000; operating expenses (OPEX) 5,000.
Year B: revenue 22,000; COGS 14,300; OPEX 5,200.

Use these definitions:
- Gross profit = revenue - COGS
- Gross margin = gross profit / revenue, as a percent
- Operating profit = gross profit - OPEX
Make one table with a row for each year and these columns:
revenue, COGS, gross profit, gross margin, OPEX, operating profit.
Add a last row that shows the change from Year A to Year B.
Show the calculation for Year B gross margin so I can check it.
Do not explain causes and do not recommend anything yet.
Message AI...

Why it works

Reasons

  • It gives all the numbers and the formulas, which fixes the missing costs in Poor Prompt 1.

Why it works

Reasons

  • It gives the AI the checked numbers, so the brief cannot change them.
  • Two facts with numbers keep the brief grounded in the data.
  • The "Not verified" label separates a guess from a fact.
  • Asking for one question turns the guess into something Hana can check.
