Concept and methodSession 4.3

Concepts for this problem

Keep each concept tied to the practical decision.

Understand

Core concepts

  • Monthly time saved = minutes saved per use x monthly uses. These are estimates to test, not guaranteed savings.
  • Consider usefulness, repeat frequency, consistency, ease of reuse, and the cost of an error.
  • A high score does not remove the need to test a prompt on fresh examples.
Apply

A simple method

  • Estimate time saved; score prompts against shared criteria; select one to improve and explain the trade-off.
