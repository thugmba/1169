Concept and methodSession 3.4

Concepts for this problem

Keep each concept tied to the practical decision.

Understand

Core concepts

  • Personal or confidential data can include names, contact details, account IDs, health details, employee records, and internal business information.
  • Removing a name may not anonymize information if other details still identify a person.
  • Follow your organization's approved-tool and data-handling rules. If the rule is unknown, do not upload the data; ask the responsible person.
  • Human review is still needed: check facts, policy, tone, and whether the response exposes private details.
Apply

A simple method

  • Identify what is sensitive; check whether the tool and task are approved; minimize data and review the output before use.

Compare examplesSession 3.4

A clearer, safer prompt

Keep the source, task, constraints, and output check visible.

Use

Improved example 1

Before using AI, list the data fields in this sample that should be removed or replaced. Do not draft a reply or repeat the identifiers.
Use

Improved example 2

Assume company policy permits an approved AI tool for this task. Draft a neutral reply using only: item arrived damaged; customer asks for next steps. Ask the customer to use the secure support channel to provide order details. Do not promise a refund or repeat identifying information.
