Problem firstSession 3.4

What problem are we solving?

Learn how to use AI safely with customer messages: check the company rules, remove personal details yourself before using AI, and review every AI draft before it is sent.

The situation

The problem

Minho works in customer support at an online shop. His root problem is time: he answers about 50 customer messages a day. His idea is to let AI draft the replies. He pasted a full customer message into a free AI chat tool: "Hi, this is Daniel Lee (account 84721). I'm having an issue with my recent invoice 3029. I think I was charged twice. Please call me at 555-0184." The message contained 4 personal details, and the tool was not approved by his company. The AI reply also repeated the account number and promised, "We have refunded the double charge," which no one had checked. Minho broke two company rules because he did not check what the message contained before he pasted it.

Step 1

Check the rules first

Is the AI tool approved? What must never be pasted?

Step 2

Remove personal details yourself

Mark every personal detail and replace it with a placeholder before anything goes into AI. Do not ask the AI to remove them for you.

Step 3

Draft, then review

Use AI only with the safe summary, then check the draft against a short review checklist before sending.

Part 1Session 3.4

Five privacy words you need first

Before you use AI with customer messages, you must know these words. Each example uses Daniel Lee's message.

Key idea

Personal data

Any information about a specific person. Example: a name, a phone number, or a health condition.

Key idea

Identifier

A detail that points to one person or one account. Example: account 84721 or invoice 3029.

Key idea

Data minimization

Use only the information the task needs. To draft a reply, the AI needs "charged twice," not the phone number.

Part 1Session 3.4

Five privacy words you need first (continued)

Before you use AI with customer messages, you must know these words. Each example uses Daniel Lee's message.

Key idea

Placeholder

A label that stands in for a removed detail. Example: [CUSTOMER] instead of "Daniel Lee." You add the real detail back only after the draft is reviewed.

Key idea

Approved tool

An AI tool that your company has checked and allowed for work data. A free public chat tool is not approved unless the company says so.

Improved prompt 2 - copy and send
AI Chat
You
Review your reply with this checklist. Answer yes or no for each item:
1. Does it contain a name, phone number, email, account ID, or invoice number?
2. Does it promise anything we have not checked, such as a refund?
3. Is it polite and under 80 words?
4. Does it tell the customer the next step?
If any answer shows a problem, rewrite the reply to fix it.
Message AI...
Why it works

Reasons

  • The checklist turns "review the draft" into four clear yes/no checks.
  • Items 1 and 2 match the two mistakes in Minho's first reply.
  • Asking the AI to rewrite saves time, but the person still reads the final reply (Rule 4).
  • The same checklist works for every message, so it can become a habit.
