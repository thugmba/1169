Module 1 | Applied AI in BusinessSession 1.4

The business problem comes first

How can the store reduce peak-hour queue time while protecting service, cost, and risk?

The problem

The queue is too long.

  • Average peak-hour queue: 7.5 minutes.
  • The store handles 84 transactions per hour.
  • Customers still need accessible service.

Do not jump to one answer

Several methods may help.

  • Keep staffed lanes open.
  • Add clearer kiosk instructions.
  • Place an employee near the kiosks.
  • Test two additional kiosks in a small pilot.

How CoT helps

Compare methods before choosing one.

  • Separate facts from assumptions.
  • Use the same criteria for each method.
  • Test the most promising option before scaling.

1 | Chain-of-Thought (CoT)Session 1.4

What CoT does in this class

CoT is a structured decision rationale. It helps students inspect the basis for a recommendation.

Start with a question

State the business problem.

  • What must improve?
  • What constraints matter?
  • What counts as success?

Ask for a structure

Make the decision logic visible.

  • Facts provided.
  • Assumptions to test.
  • Options and trade-offs.

Use the result carefully

CoT is not proof.

  • Check facts against the dataset.
  • Ask for missing information.
  • Test before scaling.

Bad prompt: false certainty

Do not ask the model to guarantee a forecast that your data cannot support.

Prompt to avoid

We have 12 employees.
Tell us exactly how much
profit two kiosks will add.

Why it fails

  • No cost or traffic data.
  • Guarantees are impossible.
  • It rewards invented numbers.

Exercise 2: Simple pilot review

After 90 days, the average peak-hour queue is 6.5 minutes. The target was five minutes or less.

Step 1

Name the problem

  • Queue time is above target.
  • Customers need help at busy times.

Step 2

Choose one action

  • Place one employee near kiosks.
  • Add a short instruction sign.
  • Keep one staffed lane open.

Step 3

Measure again

  • Track queue time for two weeks.
  • Target: five minutes or less.
  • State whether the action worked.
