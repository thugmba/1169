Situation

The problem

A restaurant owner has lunch sales and customer notes, but does not know which small change could improve service without hurting profit. The table contains clues, not a ready-made answer.

Target

Learning goal

Read the table, calculate and verify revenue and gross profit, separate facts from interpretations, and propose one small test.

Concept and methodSession 2.1

Concepts for this problem

Keep each concept tied to the practical decision.

Understand

Core concepts

  • A row is one item sold on one day. A column is one kind of information.
  • Revenue = units sold x unit price.
  • Gross profit = units sold x (unit price - unit food cost). It subtracts food cost only, not wages, rent, or all other business costs.
  • Customer notes are clues. Two queue notes do not prove what caused the queue.
  • AI can organize notes and explain supplied results. A spreadsheet should calculate and verify financial values.
Apply

A simple method

  • Read the fields and gaps; calculate and check the totals; explain what the evidence supports, name what is uncertain, and choose one small test.

Compare examplesSession 2.1

A clearer, safer prompt

Keep the source, task, constraints, and output check visible.

Use

Improved example 1

Use only the attached Session 2.1 CSV. List what each column measures. Separate facts visible in the rows from questions the data cannot answer. Do not calculate totals or claim a cause. End with three questions for the owner.
Use

Improved example 2

Use the eight Session 2.1 rows and these spreadsheet-checked results: total revenue $1,016.00, gross profit $633.60, Burger gross profit $313.20. Give one demand fact, one profit fact, and one service clue. Suggest one small two-week test and one measure. Do not recalculate. Label facts and interpretations separately.

Guided practice

Exercise 1: Follow the steps

  • Open the CSV in Excel or Google Sheets. Columns A-F are day, item, units, unit price, unit food cost, and note. In G2 enter =C2D2 for revenue; in H2 enter =C2(D2-E2) for gross profit. Fill both through row 9. In G10 enter =SUM(G2:G9); in H10 enter =SUM(H2:H9). Check: revenue $1,016.00; gross profit $633.60; Burger gross profit $313.20. Then run Improved Prompt 2 and label each claim fact or interpretation.
