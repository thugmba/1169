Root problem

The plan keeps growing

Part 1 | Project wordsSession 2.3

Scope, budget, and buffer

Before you ask an AI to plan a project, know what these words mean.

Word 1

Scope

The work included in this version of the project.

  • In scope: drip coffee and tea.
  • Out of scope: work saved for later, such as an espresso machine.
Word 2

Budget and buffer

The budget is the most money the project can spend. The buffer is the money left after the planned costs.

$4,000 - $3,200 = $800 buffer

Part 1 | Project wordsSession 2.3

Milestone, dependency, and TBD

Each example uses the coffee cart.

Word 3

Milestone

A checkable result by a certain week.

Week 2: permit application submitted

You can answer yes or no.

Word 4

Dependency

Something the team needs from another person or office.

The cart cannot open until the campus approves the space.

Word 5

TBD

To be determined: a fact the team still needs to confirm.

TBD means "find out," not "guess."

How the three-step method fixes the plan

The instructor runs the prompts below and compares the answers.

Step 1 fixes

The boundary problem

  • The AI knows the budget, deadline, team, and version 1 menu.
Step 2 fixes

The trust problem

  • A cost table with a total lets you check the math in one minute.
  • AI tools can add numbers wrong. Always check the total.
Step 3 fixes

The "yes to everything" problem

  • Each new idea is checked for cost, time, and approvals.
  • Unknowns stay TBD.

Poor prompt 1 - do not use
AI Chat
You
We are a student club. Write a complete plan for opening a coffee cart on campus.
Message AI...
What the AI often answers

A typical result

A long, professional plan with an espresso machine, a large menu, a marketing campaign, and an opening date in about four weeks.

Why it is weak

Reasons

  • It gives no budget, deadline, team size, or menu, so the AI plans an ideal cart instead of this club's cart.
  • It does not ask the AI to mark unknown facts, so the AI fills them with guesses, such as an approval date.
  • The result looks finished, but it is $1,700.00 over budget when you use the club's real quotes.

Reasons

Reasons

Improved prompt 2

Same project facts. Compare what the prompt asks for.

Improved prompt 2 - copy and send
AI Chat
You
I checked your table. The version 1 total is $3,200, and the buffer is $800.
Now write a one-page project outline with:
- The launch outcome in one sentence
- Included work and excluded work
- Four milestones, each with a target week and a yes/no check
- One dependency and one risk
- The next action for this week
Keep the $4,000 budget and the 8-week target visible.
Mark every unknown date as TBD. Do not promise an approval date.
Message AI...
Why it works

Reasons

  • It gives the checked total, so the outline cannot quietly go over budget.
  • Milestones with a target week and a yes/no check make progress easy to measure.
  • Asking for one dependency and one risk shows what could delay the cart.
  • "Mark every unknown date as TBD" stops the AI from inventing an approval date.

Say yes only if it fits

  • Recommend: accept now, save for version 2, or replace an item.
