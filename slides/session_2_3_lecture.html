Lesson explanationSession 2.3

Learn

Make the project boundary visible

  • The coffee cart's first version has a $4,000 maximum budget and an eight-week target. Drip coffee and tea are in the initial menu. Fresh juice is not automatically included just because someone requests it. A scope statement names what the team will deliver now and what it will not deliver in this version. This prevents new ideas from quietly consuming the same limited time and money.
Learn

Do not turn unknowns into plan facts

  • Campus space and food approval are required, but applications are not filed. Equipment prices and approval timing are unknown. Mark these as "To confirm"; do not invent a price or a guaranteed approval date. A dependency is something the team needs from another person or office. A risk is an uncertain event that may affect the plan. Identify who can confirm an unknown and what work depends on the answer.

Learn

Evaluate a change request against the boundary

  • If a student asks to add fresh juice two weeks before opening, check its effect on budget, schedule, equipment, approval, and labor before accepting it. The supplied facts do not confirm refrigeration, extra staff, or approval. A reasonable response is to defer the request or replace an existing menu item only after checking the missing requirements. Make a bounded recommendation instead of promising that every request can fit.
Apply

Three-step method

  • State the outcome, time, and budget; separate confirmed facts, exclusions, and unknowns; choose a few checkable milestones and identify the first dependency or risk.
