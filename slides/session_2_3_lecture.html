Situation

The problem

A student club wants to open a campus coffee cart in eight weeks with a $4,000 budget. New requests can consume time and money the team does not have.

Target

Learning goal

Draft a small project outline that distinguishes included work, excluded work, unknown information, milestones, and dependencies.

Concept and methodSession 2.3

Concepts for this problem

Keep each concept tied to the practical decision.

Understand

Core concepts

  • Scope defines work included in this version. Out of scope names excluded work. A milestone is a dated, checkable result. A dependency is something needed from another person or group. A risk is an uncertain event that could affect delivery.
Apply

A simple method

  • State the outcome, time, and budget; separate confirmed facts, exclusions, and unknowns; choose a few checkable milestones and identify the first dependency or risk.

Compare examplesSession 2.3

A clearer, safer prompt

Keep the source, task, constraints, and output check visible.

Use

Improved example 1

Use only the scenario facts below. List confirmed facts and missing information. Do not estimate prices or approval dates. Ask exactly three questions that would change the launch plan.
Use

Improved example 2

Draft a one-page project outline with launch outcome, included work, excluded work, three checkable milestones, one dependency, one risk, and a next action. Keep the $4,000 limit and eight-week target visible. Mark unknown prices or dates TBD. Do not invent facts.

How to know the work is complete

Check the output before sharing or acting on it.

Submit

Deliverable

  • A short scope update stating the request, its likely effect on time, cost, or approval, and one feasible next step.
Verify

Success check

  • The plan preserves the budget and deadline, marks unknowns clearly, and lists milestones that can be checked.
