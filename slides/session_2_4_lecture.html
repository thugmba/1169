What problem are we solving?

3 hours: 50-minute lesson, 10-minute break, 50-minute guided practice, 10-minute break, 50-minute applied practice, and 10-minute wrap-up.

Situation

The problem

A team is making a budgeting app but does not know what different potential users need or what might stop them from trying it. Profiles based only on age or stereotypes can produce generic messages.

Target

Learning goal

Use short interview evidence to build two distinct, testable customer profiles and write one relevant message for each.

Lesson explanationSession 2.4

Understand the idea and apply it

Work through the explanation before comparing prompts or starting the exercise.

Learn

Build profiles from what people actually said

  • Start with an observed situation and a stated need. One note says work shifts change and the person wants to know what can be spent before Friday. Another says bill dates are forgotten and an early reminder would help. These are different needs. Do not add age, income, family status, or personality traits that are not in the notes.
Learn

Separate a user need from a product claim

  • A job to be done describes progress a person wants in a situation. A barrier explains what makes that progress difficult. The available app facts are manual entry, a weekly view, and bill reminders. Bank linking is not available and price is unset, so a profile or message must not claim either feature.

Lesson explanationSession 2.4

Understand the idea and apply it

Learn

Check whether each message is relevant and supportable

  • Connect one stated need to an available feature. Do not claim guaranteed savings or imply that a few interviewees represent a whole market. Add one unanswered question to each profile, such as what tracking takes today or when a reminder is useful. Those questions guide the next interview instead of being filled with guesses.
Apply

Three-step method

  • Identify what each person wants and what makes it difficult; group notes with similar needs while preserving differences; draft a message and check it against product facts and source notes.
